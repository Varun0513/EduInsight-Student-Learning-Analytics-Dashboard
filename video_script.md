# EduInsight - 2 Minute Video Walkthrough Script

**Target Duration:** ~2 Minutes (approx. 270 words)
**Pacing:** Conversational but professional (roughly 130-140 words per minute)

---

### [0:00 - 0:20] Introduction & The Problem (20 seconds)
**(Visual Idea:** Title screen "EduInsight", then briefly show a crowded classroom or a teacher overwhelmed with grading spreadsheets.**)**

**Speaker:** 
"Hello! Welcome to EduInsight, our submission for the Praxis 2.0 Hackathon. Today, educators face a massive challenge: managing classrooms of up to sixty students without scalable tools to understand *how* each student learns or *why* some fall behind. The result? A one-size-fits-all approach where diverse learners often fall through the cracks."

### [0:20 - 0:35] The Solution (15 seconds)
**(Visual Idea:** Screen-recording transitions to the EduInsight Dashboard top-level overview, panning across the main metrics.**)**

**Speaker:** 
"To solve this, we built EduInsight—a client-side analytics dashboard that transforms raw student behavioral data into actionable classroom intelligence. It runs entirely in your browser, keeping data local and blazing fast."

### [0:35 - 1:15] Working Prototype Walkthrough (40 seconds)
**(Visual Idea:** Highlight different sections of the dashboard as they are mentioned (Personas, Risk Radar, Strategy Recommender, Batch Upload).**)**

**Speaker:** 
"Let’s walk through the prototype. 
First, our built-in ML engine groups students into **5 Learner Personas** using K-Means clustering, giving teachers a breakdown of behavioral signatures like 'Driven Achievers' or 'Struggling Learners'.
Next is the **Risk Radar**. Using a 10-point disengagement model, it flags high-risk students *before* they fail, so teachers know exactly who needs help today.
When a teacher selects a student, our **Strategy Recommender** instantly provides expert-informed instructional strategies tailored to that specific persona. 
And to make it completely frictionless, teachers can use our **Batch CSV Upload** to predict risk levels for an entire class instantly."

### [1:15 - 1:45] Key Insights (30 seconds)
**(Visual Idea:** Scroll to the distinct charts showing Feature Impact and Correlation Analysis.**)**

**Speaker:** 
"Analyzing over six thousand student records revealed some surprising insights. We found that *Attendance* and *Motivation Level* are actually much stronger predictors of success than raw study hours. Furthermore, our models show that just over 20% of 'Struggling Learners' account for 50% of all high-risk cases—pinpointing exactly where early intervention is needed most."

### [1:45 - 2:00] Conclusion (15 seconds)
**(Visual Idea:** Zoom out to show the full dashboard, then transition to a final title card or team logo.**)**

**Speaker:** 
"By replacing hours of manual grade analysis with instant, ML-driven insights, EduInsight empowers educators to personalize their teaching and ensure no student is left behind. Thank you for watching!"
